What I am known for

Most Dynamics 365 projects end up at the same question:

How do customers and partners get to the data without seeing more than they are allowed to?

That has been my focus since 2014. I plan the data model, the internal security roles and the permissions for external users together from the start. That way the portal is not an afterthought but part of the solution.
