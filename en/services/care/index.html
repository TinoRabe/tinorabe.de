Users sign in every day, data from Dynamics 365 goes outside every day, and Microsoft changes the platform every month. Care gives your portal someone who is responsible: I look at it regularly, assess every relevant change and record in writing what I have found.

What happens every month

Security and configuration review
Table permissions, web roles, site settings and sign-in are checked systematically. Deviations are noticed before anyone exploits them.
Microsoft release check with assessment
I read the release notes, you get the assessment: what affects your portal, what needs to be done, what you can ignore.
Written monthly report
Status, findings, recommendations, open items. Also for audit and compliance purposes.
Small changes included
A bug, a permission, a form. Anything bigger we agree on beforehand.

Frequently asked questions

Why not just call when something breaks?
Because the dangerous problems do not look broken. A portal with a table permission that is scoped too widely works perfectly, for your users and for everyone else.
A partner built our portal. Does Care still fit?
Yes. The starting point is then usually a security audit as the baseline assessment, after which Care takes over.
What counts as a small change?
Whatever can realistically be done within the monthly allowance. I estimate bigger projects beforehand, so you decide consciously.
