    Understanding the Licensing Model

    Authenticated Users vs. Anonymous Visitors

    Authenticated Users

    Definition: people with a login account who see personalized data.

    Examples:

    • B2B customer views their orders
    • Partner views their commission statement
    • Supplier views open purchase orders

    Licensing: per active user/month

    Anonymous Visitors

    Definition: people without a login who only see public content.

    Examples:

    • Prospect reads the Knowledge Base
    • Visitor browses FAQs
    • Public product documentation

    Licensing: unlimited in the base license

    Important: only ACTIVE authenticated users per month are licensed, not registered accounts. If you have 1,000 registered customers but only 400 log in during January, you pay for 400 users.

    Hidden Costs? What Else May Apply

    1 Capacity Add-Ons (for high traffic)

    What is it? additional compute and storage capacity for very large portals.

    When do I need this?

    • More than 50,000 API calls per day
    • More than 1 GB file storage
    • Complex business logic with many automations

    Costs: from €45/month (50,000 requests)

    Most mid-market portals do NOT need Capacity Add-Ons.

    2 Premium Connectors (for third-party systems)

    What is it? licenses for connections to non-Microsoft systems (e.g. SAP, Salesforce, Oracle).

    Examples of Premium Connectors:

    • SAP ERP
    • Salesforce CRM
    • Oracle Database
    • Adobe Sign

    Costs: from €40/user/month (Power Automate Premium)

    For Microsoft systems (Dynamics 365, SharePoint, SQL) NO Premium Connectors are needed.

    3 Azure Services (Optional)

    What is it? additional cloud services for specialized functions (e.g. AI, custom APIs).

    Examples:

    • Azure Blob Storage (large file uploads)
    • Azure Functions (custom backend logic)
    • Azure AI Services (chatbot, OCR)

    Costs: variable, typically €20-100/month

    Only needed for very specialized requirements.

    4 External Developer Support (Optional)

    What is it? maintenance, updates and new features delivered by an external service provider.

    Typical support packages:

    • 5 hours/month: approx. €500/month
    • 10 hours/month: approx. €900/month
    • On-demand (without retainer): €100-150/hour

    Many companies manage Power Pages completely in-house (that's the point of low-code).

    Reality for 80% of mid-market B2B portals:
    costs = base license + authenticated users. No Capacity Add-Ons, no Premium Connectors, no Azure. Simple, transparent, predictable.

    Quick Calculator: Your Licenses

    How to calculate your monthly costs:

    1. 1. Count your B2B customers who need a login (e.g. 500)
    2. 2. Multiply by 0.5 (activity rate) = 250 active users/month
    3. 3. Subtract 100 (included in the base license) = 150 additional users
    4. 4. Divide by 100 and round up = 2 user packs
    5. 5. Calculate: €200 (base) + (2 × €317) = €834/month
