What Problems Does Power Pages Solve?

Discover how Power Pages solves the challenge of integrating external participants into your business processes without complex M365 onboarding overhead.

Contents

    If you're a Power Platform or Dynamics 365 solution owner, you've likely already implemented valuable business processes for your M365 users. But have you considered how external participants - customers, clients, partners, or vendors - could benefit from joining these processes?

    This guide explains how Power Pages solves this exact challenge: providing a secure, accessible way for external participants to engage with your business processes without the complexity of full M365 onboarding.

    The Challenge: External Participants in Business Processes

    Imagine this scenario: you've built powerful business processes in Dynamics 365 or Power Platform that work seamlessly for your internal teams. But parts of these processes could be far more efficient if external participants could engage with them directly, instead of routing every step back through your internal staff.

    The Traditional Approach

    Option 1: Onboard as M365 Users

    Each external participant gets a full M365 license and access to your internal systems.

    • Full access to all features
    • Complex onboarding process
    • Overkill for simple tasks

    Option 2: Manual Coordination

    Continue with phone calls, emails, and spreadsheets.

    • No licensing costs
    • Time-consuming
    • Error-prone
    • No real-time visibility

    Both approaches have significant drawbacks. The first is too expensive and complex for what's often a handful of simple, recurring interactions. The second lacks efficiency and gives you no real-time collaboration capabilities at all.

    The Power Pages Solution

    Power Pages provides the perfect middle ground: a tailored, secure user interface that's accessible from the public internet. External participants can engage with your business processes as if they were internal users - but without the complexity and cost of M365 onboarding.

    How It Works

    1. 1.
      Create a Power Pages portal with access to your Dataverse data
    2. 2.
      Set up appropriate security roles and permissions
    3. 3.
      Invite external participants via email
    4. 4.
      External users authenticate and access only what they need

    The result: external participants can now interact with your business processes directly through a web interface, with the same level of security and data integrity as your internal users.

    Key Benefits of Power Pages Integration

    Reduced Communication Overhead

    • No more phone calls to give or collect information
    • No more email threads with long circulation times
    • All communication happens within the portal

    Real-Time Visibility

    • External participants can track progress anytime, anywhere
    • No more "Where are we in the process?" questions
    • Complete audit trail of all interactions

    Improved Security

    • Granular control over what external users can access
    • No need to grant full M365 access
    • All interactions are logged and auditable

    Cost-Effective

    • No need for expensive M365 licenses
    • Reduced operational costs from fewer support calls
    • Flexible pricing model based on your needs

    Key Insight

    Power Pages transforms external participants from passive recipients of information into active contributors to your business processes - without the complexity and cost of traditional integration methods.

    Bonus: Works with Azure SQL and SharePoint Too

    Here's the best part: you don't need to be a Power Platform expert to benefit from Power Pages.

    Use Cases Without Power Platform Experience

    Azure SQL Database
    Create a public-facing interface for your Azure SQL data without exposing the database directly to the internet
    SharePoint Lists
    Build a custom portal for external stakeholders to view and update SharePoint list data
    Legacy Systems
    Connect to legacy systems via APIs and provide a modern web interface

    Power Pages can serve as the front-facing website for any data source, making it an incredibly versatile tool for integrating external participants into your business processes.

    Important Consideration

    While Power Pages is powerful, it's not a replacement for every custom development scenario. For highly specialized requirements or very high traffic volumes, a custom solution might still be necessary. For the vast majority of business process integration needs, though, Power Pages provides an excellent balance of functionality, security, and cost-effectiveness.
