Career

I am a trained IT systems management assistant (IT-Systemkaufmann) and hold a Bachelor of Arts in Business Informatics (FH Kiel). In 2014 I came to Dynamics CRM and Adxstudio portals at Avanade; my first portal project was a B2B customer service portal for a telecommunications group. After that I delivered Dynamics 365 projects with Sales, Customer Service and Field Service at ascendit, proMX and adesso, often with a portal for customers or partners. I have followed the platform through every rename: Dynamics 365 Portals, Power Apps Portals, Power Pages. Since 2025 I have been working freelance.
